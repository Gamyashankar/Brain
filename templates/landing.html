Explainable AI

Don't just predict. Explain.

Understanding why an AI model makes a prediction is as important as the prediction itself.

GRAD-CAM

Highlights regions that contribute strongly to the model's prediction.

SHAP

Provides region-level contribution explanations via Shapley values.

LIME

Builds a local interpretable model to explain which super-pixels mattered most.

Medical education

Know the signs of stroke

Use the FAST method to recognize stroke symptoms quickly.

FFace

Ask the person to smile. Does one side of the face droop?

AArm

Ask them to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward?

SSpeech

Ask them to repeat a sentence. Is speech slurred or strange?

TTime

Time to call emergency services — every minute counts.

If stroke symptoms are suspected, seek emergency medical attention immediately. This is educational content, not an AI diagnosis.
