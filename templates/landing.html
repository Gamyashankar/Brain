What we detect

Three clinical outcomes

Every scan is classified into one of three categories, each with its own explainability view.

Normal

No stroke signature detected. The scan is classified as healthy brain tissue.

Ischemic Stroke

Blocked blood vessel — a subtle low-density region is highlighted by the model.

Hemorrhagic Stroke

Bleeding in the brain — a bright hyperdense region is flagged as high severity.

The pipeline

From scan to report in five steps

1 · Register patient

The doctor creates a patient record with medical history and demographics.

2 · Upload scan

An axial CT or MRI slice is uploaded; SAM segments the brain region of interest.

3 · Classify

The Swin Transformer scores the scan as normal, ischemic, or hemorrhagic.

4 · Explain

LIME, Grad-CAM, and SHAP build a consensus heatmap of the affected region.

5 · Report

A branded clinical PDF is generated, stored, and made available to doctor and patient.

The AI engine

A vision backbone built for medical imaging

The Swin Transformer processes the SAM-segmented brain region in overlapping windows, while LIME, Grad-CAM, and SHAP converge on the exact regions the model used to decide.
